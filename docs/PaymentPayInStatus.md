# PaymentPayInStatus

The business status of a pay-in transaction: - `Detected`: The incoming transaction has been detected but the funds are not yet available. - `PendingScreening`: The incoming transaction is awaiting a screening result. - `ActionRequired`: Action from the customer is required before processing can continue. - `Completed`: The funds are available for the corresponding Payments business object. - `Failed`: The incoming transaction cannot be credited.
