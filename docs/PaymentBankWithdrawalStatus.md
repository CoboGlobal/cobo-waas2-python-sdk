# PaymentBankWithdrawalStatus

The status of the bank withdrawal: - `Pending`: The bank withdrawal has been created and is awaiting processing. - `Processing`: The bank withdrawal is being processed. - `Completed`: The bank withdrawal has been completed. - `Failed`: The bank withdrawal failed.
